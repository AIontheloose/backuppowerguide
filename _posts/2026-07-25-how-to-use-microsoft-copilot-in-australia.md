## Getting It Running on Australian Gear & Networks

Getting the thing to work isn’t just about typing “buy” into a browser. Microsoft locks Copilot behind specific OS builds and app versions. Here’s how to bypass the friction without calling support.

### Windows 11 & Office Mobile Setup
On desktop, you need Windows 11 version 23H2 or later with the latest preview channel updates. Open any Office app—Word, Excel, or Outlook—and look for the Copilot pane on the right. If it’s greyed out, your account isn’t linked to a paid subscription. For mobile, grab the Microsoft 365 (Office) app from the App Store or Google Play. Log in with the same M365 account. The AI prompt sits at the top of the home screen. I’ve found the mobile version surprisingly competent for drafting emails on the train through Sydney’s CBD, provided you’re not expecting it to rewrite a 40-page thesis in one go. Mobile sync relies heavily on background data, so pair your setup with quality peripherals that won’t drop connections mid-prompt: [Wireless Keyboard & Mouse Combo](https://www.amazon.com.au/s?k=wireless+keyboard+mouse&tag=owlno-22).

### The NBN Reality Check
Let’s address the elephant in the room: regional connectivity. Copilot’s AI model runs on Microsoft’s cloud, meaning your actual device specs matter less than your upload/download stability. According to recent ACMA benchmarks, urban fibre connections in Sydney and Melbourne average 50–100 Mbps downlink, which handles Copilot’s cloud token streaming without stutter. Regional Queensland or WA setups relying on NBN Fixed Wireless often sit at 10–25 Mbps uplink. That’s enough for basic drafting, but AI image generation or large document summarisation will time out. If you’re in a fringe zone, stick to text-based prompts and disable auto-save while working offline. ## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Copilot available for Australian students at a discount?**
Yes, but only through official educational licensing channels rather than standard consumer stores. Universities and registered TAFEs can provision discounted M365 Education plans that include limited Copilot access, though full AI features often require institutional approval. Always verify your eligibility via your campus IT portal before attempting to purchase a personal subscription, as student discounts don’t apply to retail checkout flows.

**How does GST appear on my Microsoft 365 invoice?**
Every Australian payment includes 10% GST baked into the displayed monthly price. Your bank statement or digital receipt will show a single AUD total without a separate tax line item, which matches standard Australian consumer billing practices. If you require an official tax invoice for business deductions, download it directly from your Microsoft account dashboard under “Payment & Billing” rather than relying on automated email receipts.

**Can I use Copilot offline on my Windows 11 PC?**
No. Copilot requires an active internet connection to route prompts through Microsoft’s cloud servers for processing. You can draft documents locally, but any AI generation, summarisation, or formatting commands will fail without bandwidth. If your regional NBN drops during a critical workflow, switch to manual editing until connectivity restores. Offline mode only caches previously generated content; it doesn’t run the model locally.

**Which plan should I choose if I work from home with a partner?**
The Family plan at $32/month is the clear winner for dual-income households. It provides five separate Microsoft accounts, meaning each person gets independent Copilot history, private drafts, and dedicated workspace boundaries. Splitting the cost drops the effective price to under $6.50 per user, which heavily undercuts standalone productivity software or competing AI add-ons. Avoid merging your logins into a single account just to save cash; you’ll lose data privacy and risk accidental file overwrites during collaborative sessions.

## The Verdict: Is Copilot Worth Your Hard-Earned AUD?

Australian users must account for regional NBN connectivity, verify GST-inclusive billing, and avoid the corporate upsell trap by sticking to consumer tiers unless managing a registered business. If you’re solo, grab the Personal plan. If you share a roof with at least one other active office user, lock in the Family tier immediately. Cancel free trials through the official portal, secure your account credentials, and stop waiting for the AI hype to fade—it’s here, it’s priced transparently, and it only pays for itself when you actually press the right buttons.
