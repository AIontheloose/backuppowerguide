### Verify Your Coverage Before You Spend a Cent
Before you even crack open a toolbox or book an appointment, check your warranty status with cold precision. AppleCare+ in Australia runs $149 AUD annually, but let’s be clear: it does not extend free cracked-screen repairs. For iPhones, you pay a $29.99 AUD deductible per accidental damage incident. If your device is eligible, head straight to an Apple Store or an Apple Authorised Service Provider (AASP). They’ll swap the assembly, restore water resistance, and keep your device’s service history intact. 

Samsung flagships like the S24 series carry a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty, but it explicitly excludes physical damage and liquid ingress. ### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a third-party repair shop without voiding my warranty?**  
Only if the work stays outside Samsung’s sealed chassis and doesn’t trigger internal damage indicators. Once you replace non-OEM components or open the device, standard coverage is typically voided for any related faults.

**How long does Samsung’s factory warranty actually last?**  
Flagship models like the S24 series come with a 12-month limited warranty from the date of purchase. It covers manufacturing defects only and explicitly excludes physical damage, water exposure, or normal wear and tear.

**What counts as "liquid ingress" under Australian consumer law?**  
Samsung uses internal Liquid Contact Indicators (LCIs) that permanently change colour when exposed to moisture. Under the ACL, manufacturers must honour claims for genuine defects, but they can legally deny coverage if corrosion or water damage is confirmed—unless you can prove a manufacturing flaw in the device’s sealing process.

**Is it cheaper to repair or replace a cracked S24 screen?**  
A genuine Samsung screen replacement usually runs $350–$550 AUD depending on the model and region. If your phone is over two years old and shows secondary issues like battery swelling or charging port corrosion, replacement often makes more financial sense than chasing another patch job.

**Do extended retail warranties cover battery degradation?**  
Generally, no. Major retailers extend coverage for accidental damage and technical failures, but battery wear is classified as a consumable. Always review your specific Protection Insurance Product disclosure statement to confirm health thresholds and claim limits.
