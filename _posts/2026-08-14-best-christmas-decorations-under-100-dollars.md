### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**When should I order my Christmas decorations to ensure delivery before the holidays?**
To avoid peak-season delays, especially with Amazon AU and other local retailers, aim to order your decorations by early November. While some items may arrive in time, ordering a few weeks ahead guarantees you have everything ready to go before the festive rush hits.

**Are inflatable decorations safe for windy Aussie summers?**
Yes, provided you secure them correctly. Look for models with sturdy stakes and sandbags included, as mentioned with the Inflatable Santa. Always check the weather forecast; if a gale is predicted, it's wise to deflate and store large inflatables temporarily to prevent damage or safety hazards.

**How do I maintain battery-operated lights for multiple Christmas seasons?**
Remove batteries during storage to prevent corrosion. Use high-quality alkaline or rechargeable batteries for consistent brightness. When storing, wrap cables gently around a cardboard tube or use the original packaging to prevent tangling and wire damage. Label your storage bins clearly to make setup next year much easier!
