### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How early should I book to secure a spot?**
A: Based on the deadlines above, you'll want to act quickly! Mid-year is peak booking season for Australian camps. Programs with subsidies, like Wunambal Cultural Immersion, often fill up within days of opening registration. For the STEM and Adventure camps, aim to book at least three weeks before their respective deadlines to avoid disappointment.

**Q: Are financial assistance options available beyond the subsidised camp?**
A: While the Wunambal program offers a significant subsidy for Indigenous families and community groups, other camps may offer sibling discounts, early-bird reductions, or flexible payment plans. I recommend emailing the camp coordinators directly; many are happy to work with families facing financial hardship to ensure access remains inclusive.

**Q: How do I choose the right camp for my child's temperament?**
A: Consider their interests and comfort zone. If your child loves hands-on discovery, *Little Innovators STEM Lab* or *Coral Coast Marine Discovery* will keep them engaged. For those who thrive in nature and need a break from screens, *Bushwackers Junior Expedition* provides excellent outdoor challenges. *Wunambal Cultural Immersion* is perfect for families seeking deep cultural connection and storytelling.

**Q: What should I look for regarding safety?**
A: All reputable camps listed here should have qualified first-aiders, clear staff-to-campion ratios, and robust risk management policies. When enrolling, ask about accommodation hygiene, emergency protocols, and how they handle homesickness. Don't hesitate to request a copy of their safety manual before signing up.
