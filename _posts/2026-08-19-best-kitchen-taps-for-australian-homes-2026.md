> **Sarah's Maintenance Hacks: Keeping Your Tap Sparkling**
> Hard water is the silent enemy of stainless steel taps, especially here in Perth and Adelaide. I've found that maintaining your tap is easy but often overlooked. Here's my mini-maintenance routine to extend your tap's life:
> *   **Daily Wipe:** After installation and periodically thereafter, wipe down the spout with a microfiber cloth and a drop of lemon juice to dissolve limescale. Let it sit for 10 minutes before rinsing for stubborn spots.
> *   **Vinegar Soak:** For heavy buildup on the aerator, unscrew the head and soak it in white vinegar for 30 minutes.
> *   **Sealant Check:** Every six months, check the silicone seal at the base. Cracks let water seep underneath, causing rust stains on your benchtop that are a nightmare to remove.
> Properly maintained stainless-steel kitchen taps have an average lifespan of **12 years** in Australian homes, so giving it a little love pays off long-term.

### What to Look for Before You Buy

Before we dive into the picks, let's clear up a common frustration: mounting styles and clearance. In 2026, deck-mounted taps are still king for their versatility, but ensure your sink has enough deck space. If you're swapping out an old tap, measure the hole diameter (usually 35mm) and check the height of your faucet against any backsplash or window to avoid clashes.

Also, consider your water pressure. Pull-down sprays can struggle in older homes with low pressure, whereas pull-out hoses offer more flexibility for rinsing large pots without tangling. ### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install a smart or touchless kitchen faucet myself?**  
Yes. Most modern faucets are designed for DIY installation and include clear mounting hardware and step-by-step guides. Just verify that you have adequate clearance under the sink for hoses, power modules, or battery compartments before purchasing.

**How do I know if my existing sink will fit a new faucet?**  
Measure the spacing between your current holes (typically 4″ for centerset or 8″ for widespread setups) and note the hole diameter. If you’re switching to a single-handle model, you’ll likely need an escutcheon plate to cover unused openings while maintaining a clean look.

**What maintenance do smart faucets require?**  
Smart faucets perform best with regular upkeep: replace backup batteries annually (if not hardwired), descale the aerator every 3–6 months, and wipe sensors with a dry microfiber cloth to prevent mineral or soap film from interfering with activation.

**Do I need a licensed plumber for installation?**  
If your isolation valves are accessible, pipes are in good condition, and you’re comfortable with basic hand tools, DIY is very manageable. However, if your home uses older plumbing, requires new water lines, or you’re unsure about local compliance, hiring a licensed Australian plumber ensures safety and warranty validity.
