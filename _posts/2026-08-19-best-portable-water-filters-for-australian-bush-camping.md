### 7. FAQ – Quick Answers for the Bush

#### Q1: *Do I need to pre‑condition my filter before first use?*  
**A:** Yes, especially for ceramic filters like Sawyer Squeeze and MSR Guardian Pro. Rinse with a few litres of clean water, then run several cycles until the flow stabilises. This removes dust and manufacturing residues.

#### Q2: *Can I use these filters in saltwater (e.g., during a beach camp)?*  
**A:** None of the four are designed for saline conditions. For coastal or saltwater environments, consider a dedicated marine filter like the **Grayl Ultralight**, which handles up to 10 % salinity.

#### Q3: *What’s the safest way to clean and maintain my filter?*  
**A:** After each use, rinse the filter head with fresh water. For deeper cleaning, soak in a mild soap solution, then rinse thoroughly. Replace cartridges or filters as per manufacturer guidelines—never push past the rated capacity.

#### Q4: *How do I know if my source water is safe to filter?*  
**A:** In Australia, you can refer to local water safety alerts from the **Australian Geographic Society** or your state’s Department of Water. If the source has known contamination (heavy metals, industrial runoff), a filter alone may not be sufficient; consider additional treatment like UV or chemical disinfection.
