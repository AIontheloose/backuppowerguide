## Mains: The Heart of the Feast

### Roast Turkey with a Twist: Lemon Myrtle and Macadamia Crust
For many families, turkey is the non-negotiable centrepiece. In 2026, you can grab a premium 4kg free-range raw bird from Coles for **$85.50**. Note that this is uncooked, so factor in a solid two-and-a-half hours of roasting time. To elevate this classic Roast turkey Australian style, skip the plain sage and lean into our distinct native flavours. I love crafting a crust from crushed macadamia nuts, finely grated lemon myrtle, and a gentle whisper of brown sugar. Lemon myrtle is in full swing during summer and adds a gorgeous citrus aroma that cuts through the richness of the meat perfectly.

**Prep & Cook:** 30 mins prep | 2 hrs 45 mins cook | Serves: 12–15 people
**Allergen Note:** Contains tree nuts (macadamia). Naturally gluten-free if you skip breadcrumbs in your stuffing.
**Pro Tip:** The biggest mistake I see families make is relying on a timer instead of a temperature gauge, which inevitably leads to dry, stringy meat. Poultry must reach a minimum internal temperature of 75°C to be safe. I always tell my readers: grab one of those handy digital thermometers and remove the turkey at 74°C (165°F), as it will continue to rise slightly while resting under foil. This simple step guarantees moist, juicy meat every time.

### The Classic Ham: Glazed and Ready-to-Roast
If ham is your tradition, Woolworths offers a fantastic premium grade 3kg fresh Christmas ham for **$75.00** (as of 23-Oct-2026). Retailers are now stocking more pre-brined ham for Christmas ready-to-roast options to simplify your prep, which is a lifesaver when you're juggling sides and decorations. Just because it's pre-brined doesn't mean it needs no extra flavour, though. Apply a citrus-herb rub with grated orange zest and cracked black pepper 1–2 hours before roasting to ensure the skin gets that crackling crunch and the meat is seasoned through.

**Prep & Cook:** 45 mins prep | 2 hrs cook | Serves: 10–12 people
**Allergen Note:** Contains soy (brine). Naturally gluten-free.
**Budget-Savvy Swap:** If the premium ham feels steep this year, try a gammon joint or lean into a hearty plant-based roast loaf from your local grocer. You’ll save around $25 without sacrificing that festive carving moment.

## Sides That Sing: Lighter Bites for Mid-Summer Days
Australian Christmas means mid‑summer Australia heat, so lighter sides are essential to complement heavier mains. Chilled salads and dishes that can be prepared in advance take the pressure off you on the big day.

### Fig and Watercress Salad with Lemon Myrtle Vinaigrette
Fresh figs are coming into season now, adding a sweet, jammy texture to salads. Follow these easy steps: Hull and halve 6 ripe figs, toss them with 3 cups of washed watercress and a handful of rocket leaves in a large bowl. Crumble over 150g of feta cheese. In a small jar, shake together 3 tbsp lemon myrtle-infused oil, 1 tbsp white wine vinegar, 1 tsp honey, and a pinch of sea salt. Pour over the greens just before serving.

**Prep Time:** 20 mins | Serves: 8–10
**Allergen Note:** Contains dairy (feta). Easily made vegan by swapping feta for cashew-based crumbles or omitting entirely.
[Amazon Lemon Myrtle Link](https://www.amazon.com.au/s?k=lemon+myrtle+dried+herbs&tag=owlno-22)

### Chilled Avocado and Prawn Pasta Salad
Embracing the mixed protein trend, this salad brings seafood to the table without the oven heat. Boil 400g of shell-on prawns in salted water until just pink (about 3 mins), then plunge into ice water to chill. Combine with diced avocado, cherry tomatoes halved, sweet corn kernels, and fusilli pasta tossed in a lemon-dill dressing. This fresh summer salads for Christmas option is perfect for guests who prefer seafood or vegetarian alternatives and can be made hours ahead.

**Prep Time:** 30 mins (plus chilling) | Serves: 10–12
**Allergen Note:** Contains shellfish and gluten. Swap fusilli for corn-starch pasta to keep it gluten-free.

## Sweet Endings: Pavlova and Pumpkin Tart Perfection
No Aussie Christmas is complete without dessert, and this year brings two fantastic options that cater to different cravings.

### The Ultimate Aussie Pavlova
A classic Pavlova recipe Australia remains the queen of desserts. Using a meringue mix keeps costs down while achieving professional results. The Good Food Store offers a 200g Pavlova meringue mix for just **$12.30**, and a 500g pack of fresh mixed berries from Woolworths is **$18.40**. That gives you a dessert base of roughly $30.70, which feeds a crowd beautifully. Follow the packet instructions to bake your meringue, let it cool completely in the turned-off oven, then whip 600ml of thickening cream until soft peaks form. Pile on kiwi fruit slices, passionfruit pulp, and the berries for a burst of colour and flavour.

**Prep & Cook:** 15 mins prep | 1 hr 30 mins cook | 4 hrs chilling | Serves: 12
**Allergen Note:** Contains eggs and dairy (cream). The meringue base is naturally gluten-free.
**Pro Tip:** Never forget the cold chain for your pavlova! Once assembled, chill it for at least 4 hours before serving. I've seen too many guests disappointed by a soggy base because the dessert was left out in the summer heat. Store it in a cool, dry place until the last moment, and add the cream and fruit just before you sit down to eat.
[Amazon Pavlova Mix Link](https://www.amazon.com.au/s?k=pavlova+meringue+mix+australian&tag=owlno-22)

### Savoury Pumpkin and Feta Tart with Macadamia Crust
For a savoury alternative or a hearty vegetarian main side, this pumpkin tart is a winner. Use **$8.00** worth of roasted pumpkin chunks and a ready-made pastry base for **$10.20**, whisking them with eggs, nutmeg, thyme, and feta before baking until golden. The macadamia crust adds a delightful crunch and highlights our local produce. This dish is naturally gluten-free if you opt for an almond-based pastry base, making it inclusive for all dietary needs. I recommend placing savoury tarts on the table alongside mains so guests can help themselves, reducing the rush in the kitchen.

**Prep & Cook:** 40 mins prep | 50 mins cook | Serves: 8–10
**Allergen Note:** Contains dairy and gluten (unless almond crust is used).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How accurate are these 2026 price estimates?**  
These figures reflect average Q4 pricing across Woolworths, Coles, and The Good Food Store during the October 15–23 window. Regional butchers, independent grocers, or online specialty retailers may vary by ±15%.

**Can I swap turkey for ham to save money?**  
Yes. Ham typically costs slightly less per serving and offers a forgiving cook time. Just remember that pre-brined hams are naturally salty, so tone down added salt in sides and consider a light brown sugar or mustard glaze instead of honey-based options.

**What’s the best time to buy for maximum discounts?**  
Stock up 3–4 weeks out. Supermarkets roll out “Christmas Lunch Bundles” from late November, with the steepest markdowns occurring between December 10–20. Freeze uncooked turkey up to one month ahead if you catch a mid-November sale.

**Are there allergy-safe alternatives listed?**  
Every row includes allergen notes for quick reference. Swap wheat-based stuffing for rice or quinoa blends, use dairy-free spreads in sides, and opt for nut-free pavlova or fruit-based desserts to keep the meal inclusive without sacrificing flavour.

**How far in advance should I shop?**  
Proteins: 2–3 days before cooking (or freeze uncooked turkey up to 4 weeks prior). Sides & pantry staples: 5–7 days ahead. Fresh herbs, cream, and delicate desserts: 1–2 days before the feast.
