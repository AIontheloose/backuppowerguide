### FAQ: Your Steam Mop Questions Answered
**Can steam mops damage my Australian hardwood or laminate floors?**  
Not when you choose the right model and follow basic guidelines. Always verify that your flooring manufacturer approves steam cleaning, avoid over-saturating the surface, and use a steam mop specifically designed for timber compatibility. Dyson and Hoover’s auto-adjusting systems are particularly safe because they modulate output to prevent excess moisture from seeping into plank joints.

**How often should I descale my steam mop in Australia’s varying water hardness?**  
In soft-water regions like Tasmania and coastal NSW, descaling every eight to ten weeks is typically sufficient. However, if you live in hard-water areas such as inland QLD or WA, monthly descaling with a citric acid solution will prevent mineral buildup that can clog the steam nozzle and reduce performance over time.

**Is tap water safe to use in steam mops, or should I buy distilled?**  
While many modern steam mops tolerate treated mains water, using distilled or demineralised water significantly extends the lifespan of internal heating elements and seals. If you’re committed to your steam mop long-term, investing a few dollars in distilled water bottles will save you from costly pump replacements down the track.

**What’s the difference between corded and battery-operated steam mops for Aussie homes?**  
Corded models offer unlimited runtime but require managing extension cords across multiple rooms, which can feel cumbersome in open-plan layouts. Battery-operated units provide genuine freedom of movement and are ideal for quick daily cleans or multi-level homes, though you’ll need to factor in recharging time between sessions. For most Australian households, a 45-60 minute battery life strikes the perfect balance between convenience and practicality.
