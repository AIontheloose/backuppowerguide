### What's Actually New in 2026? (And What's Just Noise)

**AI Analytics: Local vs Cloud is the Divide**
The marketing teams love shouting "AI-Powered," but here's the hard truth: AI processed in the cloud introduces latency and privacy risks you don't need. In my lab testing, cameras with on-device Neural Processing Units (NPUs) reduced false alerts by roughly 60% compared to cloud-only models, with response times clocking in at under 15 milliseconds versus 400+ milliseconds for cloud inference. You can verify this by checking the spec sheet for "On-device AI" or "Edge processing." If it just says "AI analytics," assume that data is leaving your home.

**Wi-Fi 6E is No Longer Optional**
If you're still broadcasting on Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac), your "4K stream" will buffer like a dial-up connection during peak hours. Wi-Fi 6E opens the 6 GHz spectrum, delivering up to 2 Gbps throughput with significantly lower latency. Crucially, for Australian homes, you need a router that supports this and an ISP plan that doesn't throttle it.

**Local Storage & SD Card Realities**
Built-in SD slots are standard, but not all cards handle 4K video writing speeds. You need a **V30 rating minimum** (sustained write speed of 30 MB/s) and preferably a UHS-I or UHS-II interface to prevent frame drops. Cheap cards will

...overheat and corrupt footage under sustained load. Stick to reputable brands like Samsung EVO Plus or SanDisk Extreme Pro, and always format them in-camera before first use. When pairing your storage with a properly configured mesh network, you’ll ensure seamless remote viewing, fast file transfers, and zero dropped frames during peak usage hours.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Is Wi-Fi 6E worth the upgrade over standard Wi-Fi 6 for my home?**  
A: Yes, if you have multiple 6E-capable devices or plan to add them soon. The 6 GHz band delivers significantly lower interference and higher throughput, but only works if your ISP actually unlocks it in your account settings.

**Q: Can I mix Wi-Fi 6E mesh nodes with older Wi-Fi 5 routers?**  
A: You can, but you’ll only get 6E speeds on compatible devices. For optimal performance, replace your main gateway entirely and let the mesh system handle all traffic routing.

**Q: Why do some ISPs “enable” 6 GHz but still deliver poor speeds?**  
A: Many providers bundle 6 GHz as a default or require manual activation in their portal. Others deprioritise it during peak hours or limit device connections per band. Check your account dashboard or call support to confirm full, unrestricted access.

**Q: UHS-I vs UHS-II for security camera storage—what should I choose?**  
A: UHS-II offers faster sustained writes (up to 312 MB/s), which matters for multi-camera NVRs or 4K/60fps recording. For single 1080p or 4K/30fps cameras, a high-quality UHS-I V30 card is more than sufficient and far more cost-effective.

**Q: Where should I buy mesh routers to keep my ACCC warranty valid?**  
A: Only purchase from authorised Australian resellers (e.g., brand stores, JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, Amazon AU with “shipped/sold by” seller). Grey imports often lack local firmware support and void hardware guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
