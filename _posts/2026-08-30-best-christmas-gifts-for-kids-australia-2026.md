#### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I pick games when my kids span different ages?**  
A: Look for “gateway” titles with scalable rules or cooperative play. Games like *Kingdomino* or *Just One* let younger players contribute meaningfully while older ones still face a challenge. You can also alternate turns or assign roles that match each child’s strengths.

**Q: Are digital board game apps a good substitute?**  
A: They’re convenient for solo practice or weather-bound days, but they rarely replicate the tactile joy and spontaneous banter of physical gameplay. I recommend using them as supplements, not replacements—especially when teaching rules or reviewing strategies.

**Q: What’s the smartest way to store a growing family collection?**  
A: Invest in modular storage bins with dividers, label each by expansion or mechanic, and keep frequently played games in an accessible “rotation bin.” This prevents rulebook bloat and makes setup a breeze.

**Q: How do I handle competitive frustration without shutting down game night?**  
A: Normalize losing as part of the learning curve. Use cooperative games to build teamwork, debrief lightly after losses (“What would you try next time?”), and celebrate creative moves over victories. The goal is connection, not conquest.
