### FAQ: Inflatable Pool Care & Safe Water Play

**Q: How long should I drain and dry my inflatable pool before storing it?**  
A: At least 48–72 hours of sun-drying. Even a damp pool will grow mould or develop stubborn odours over winter. Lay it flat, fold loosely (never tightly), and store in a breathable cotton sack away from direct sunlight or extreme temperature swings.

**Q: Can I use regular pool chlorine in an inflatable pool?**  
A: Yes, but keep concentrations low—0.5–1 ppm is plenty for compact volumes. Over-chlorinating degrades PVC seams faster and irritates sensitive skin. Salt-free shock treatments or bromine tablets are gentler on both material and little ones.

**Q: What’s the best way to clean algae from PVC walls?**  
A: Mix warm water with a splash of white vinegar or mild, phosphate-free dish soap. Use a soft microfiber cloth—never abrasive scrubbers or steel wool. Rinse thoroughly and let it dry completely before refilling.

**Q: Are reusable water balloons actually safe for toddlers?**  
A: Absolutely, provided they’re made from non-toxic silicone or fine mesh without small detachable components. Always supervise play, rinse them after use to prevent mould, and inspect for punctures before each refill.

**Q: How do I prevent my inflatable pool from tipping over in wind?**  
A: Anchor it with sandbags or ground stakes around the perimeter, keep the water level at least two-thirds full (water weight stabilises it), and position it away from trees or fences that could shed branches or debris.
