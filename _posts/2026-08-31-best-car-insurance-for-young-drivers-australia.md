### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is it cheaper to add me as a named driver on my parents' policy?**  
A: Occasionally, but insurers now enforce strict "primary user" declarations. If you drive the vehicle most often or live at the same address, declaring yourself as the main driver is legally required. Misrepresenting this can void your cover when you need it most.

**Q: Do I really need Comprehensive cover for a car under AUD $10,000?**  
A: For older or high-depreciation vehicles, Basic Property Damage or Third Party Fire & Theft often makes mathematical sense. However, keep Comprehensive if you live in a hail-prone region, an area with high flood risk, or a suburb with documented vandalism trends.

**Q: How long until my no-claims discount actually applies?**  
A: Most Australian insurers begin rewarding claim-free driving after 12 months. Some apply pro-rata discounts annually, while others require a full policy year before applying the reduction to renewal quotes. Always ask for the exact NCD tier structure in writing before signing.

**Q: Can I switch insurers mid-policy without penalty?**  
A: Yes, but expect a pro-rata refund minus potential early-exit fees or lost loyalty benefits. It’s almost always smarter to wait until your renewal date, gather three live quotes, and negotiate with your current insurer using the lowest competing offer as leverage.
