**Q: Which smartwatch offers the best value for Australian buyers?**  
A: For raw specs per dollar, the Oppo Watch 3 leads. If you prioritize seamless iOS integration, the Apple Watch Series 9 remains unmatched despite the higher price. The Xiaomi Mi Watch Ultra sits comfortably in the middle with solid battery life and budget-friendly pricing.

**Q: Does the Oppo Watch 3 work with iPhones?**  
A: Partially. While it pairs via Bluetooth for notifications and basic tracking, advanced health metrics and app ecosystems are optimized for Android. iOS users should stick to WatchOS or cross-platform Wear OS alternatives to avoid feature gaps.
