### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do all rugged phone cases support wireless charging?**  
A: Not automatically. Look for cases explicitly labelled “Qi-certified” or “wireless-charging compatible.” Thicker military-grade cases may reduce charging speed, but reputable brands design internal cutouts to maintain efficient power transfer.

**Q: How long should a quality phone case last in Australia’s climate?**  
A: With normal use, a well-made silicone or TPU case lasts 12–18 months before showing yellowing or stiffness. Polycarbonate or hybrid cases typically hold up longer. UV exposure and frequent washing accelerate wear, so rotate cases if possible.

**Q: Are premium cases worth the extra cost for everyday commuting?**  
A: For most users, a $30–$50 case from Spigen, Ringke, or Caseology delivers 90% of the protection at half the price of flagship brands. Save the higher budget for outdoor work, construction sites, or frequent travel where certified drop ratings matter most.

**Q: Can I return a defective phone case bought online in Australia?**  
A: Yes. Under Australian Consumer Law, faulty products must be replaced, refunded, or repaired. Reputable retailers like Amazon AU, Officeworks, and brand-direct stores offer 14–30 day hassle-free returns. Always keep your order confirmation and packaging until the return window closes.

**Q: Which brand offers the best local warranty support in 2026?**  
A: OtterBox and Spigen maintain active Australian service centres and streamlined claim portals. Caseology and Ringke rely more on retailer-mediated warranties, so purchasing through Amazon AU or JB Hi-Fi often speeds up replacements.
