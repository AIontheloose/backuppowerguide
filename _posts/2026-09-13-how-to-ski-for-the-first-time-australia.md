## FAQ: Your First-Time Family Skiing Questions, Answered

**Q: What exactly is included in the $200 bundle?**  
A: The package covers two adult half-day lift tickets, one child full-day ticket (ages 5–14), a group beginner lesson for up to four family members, thermal base/mid-layer rental sets for everyone, and a $30 meal voucher. No hidden fees—just pure slope time.

**Q: Is this suitable for kids under five or teens over fourteen?**  
A: Kids under five ski free on most resort bunnymounds with adult supervision, so they won’t need a ticket but will still get thermal gear rentals. Teens 15+ can add an adult lift ticket to the bundle for just $40 extra.

**Q: How do I book and redeem it in 2026?**  
A: Visit the SnowLink Family Portal, select “First-Timer Bundle,” choose your resort and dates, and checkout. You’ll receive a QR code via email—scan it at any staffed lift office or lesson meeting point. No printing required.

**Q: What if there’s no snow or poor weather?**  
A: Australian resorts now offer full date-shifting within 14 days of your original trip, plus a complimentary resort exploration pass (indoor climbing wall, sled park, or hot chocolate lounge) if the slopes close unexpectedly.

**Q: Can I upgrade to advanced terrain or private lessons later?**  
A: Absolutely. You can purchase upgrade passes at any base lodge using your bundle receipt for priority pricing. Private lessons are bookable separately through the same portal.

**Q: Do I need to bring my own boots and helmets?**  
A: Not if you use the thermal/gear rental add-on in the bundle. Everything is professionally fitted on arrival. Just pack warm socks, a beanie, and gloves—resorts provide the rest.
