### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will a phone mount survive a Queensland summer parked on the drive?**  
A: Only if it’s rated for continuous exposure above 50°C. Cheap ABS plastic softens and warps by December. Look for mounts with UV-stabilised polymers or aluminium housings, and always park in the shade or use a windscreen sunshade when possible.

**Q: Are magnetic mounts safe for crash testing?**  
A: Modern magnetic mounts with reinforced damping cores and secondary retention rings are rigorously tested to hold through sudden deceleration events. Clamp-style mounts can still work but often scratch dashboards and lose tension over time. I only recommend mounts that meet ISO 10542 stability benchmarks.

**Q: Do I need a wireless charging mount?**  
A: Only if you drive long distances daily. Wireless adds convenience but generates extra heat. In Australian conditions, opt for mounts with passive airflow channels or active thermal management. Otherwise, a simple magnetic mount paired with a quality car charger is more reliable long-term.

**Q: Can I legally use any mount on Australian roads?**  
A: As long as it doesn’t obstruct your forward view or interfere with airbag deployment zones. NT and QLD road rules are strict about visibility—always position your mount to the side of the windscreen or on a flat dash section, never across the driver’s line of sight.

**Q: How do I know if a brand is cutting corners?**  
A: Check for transparent material specs, crash-test documentation, and Australian customer support. If the packaging doesn’t list load ratings or thermal tolerance, or if reviews consistently mention “melting” or “slipping,” walk away. At Owlno, we only stock mounts that survive our outback trial protocol and offer genuine local warranty coverage.
