The shift is driven by a pragmatic realisation: when the grid goes down and your LPG bottle is sitting in the shed, having two fuel options isn't just a convenience—it's insurance against total powerlessness.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to switch between petrol and LPG?**  
Simply turn the fuel selector valve, allow 10–15 seconds for line purging, and pull the starter. I’ve found the transition seamless even in humid coastal conditions, though always verify regulator compatibility with your local gas supplier before connecting larger tank setups.

**Is this generator quiet enough for residential or camping use?**  
At roughly 68 dB(A) from 7 metres, it sits comfortably below standard suburban noise limits at dusk. I’ve run it during weekend getaways and street-side emergencies without drawing complaints, but placing it on a vibration mat and downwind still maximises comfort.

**What maintenance does the dual-fuel system actually require?**  
Beyond standard oil changes every 50 hours, inspect the LPG solenoid and fuel lines annually. The petrol carburetor benefits from a quality stabiliser if stored over summer, but Champion’s sealed intake design significantly reduces gumming or varnish buildup compared to older models.

**Can I safely power sensitive electronics like laptops or medical devices?**  
Yes, provided you’re using the inverter-grade variant. This chassis delivers stable voltage regulation with minimal flicker, making it suitable for most modern appliances. For truly sensitive equipment, pair it with a dedicated UPS or surge protector to guard against grid-switching transients.

**How does the 3-year warranty actually work in Australia?**  
Champion maintains authorised service hubs across NSW, Victoria, and QLD. Register your unit within 30 days via their portal, keep your receipt, and you’re covered for engine, alternator, and structural defects. Labour is handled through their partnered network, which has been responsive and transparent in my experience.
