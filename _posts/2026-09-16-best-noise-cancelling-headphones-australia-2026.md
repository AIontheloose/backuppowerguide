### The Data Behind the Hype: Lab Scores vs Australian Reality

### Practical Tips for Aussie Buyers

*   **Seal Integrity:** ANC performance degrades significantly if earcups don't form a complete seal. In Australia's varying climates, leatherette cushions can trap sweat while fabric meshes breathe better but wear faster. Rotate between pairs if you commute daily.
*   **Cleaning:** Clean seals monthly with isopropyl alcohol wipes to maintain maximum noise rejection and prevent bacterial growth.
*   **Transparency Mode Reality:** Ignore "transparency mode" marketing fluff. Most brands overprocess ambient sound, making voices sound robotic or metallic. Test this in a real Australian environment—a busy café or train platform—before buying. If voices sound unnatural, stick to physical isolation.
*   **Price-Trend Context:** With the XM6 launch, the XM5 has dropped to $549, offering better value than ever. The Bose 700 remains overpriced at $799 unless you specifically need the smart hub features. The JBL Club 950NC ($299) delivers the best **Australian dollar value**, offering competent ANC and rugged build quality for students and tradespeople, with consistency verified across Melbourne and Perth retailers.
