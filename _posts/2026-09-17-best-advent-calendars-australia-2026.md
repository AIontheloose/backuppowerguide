### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When do these Advent Calendars typically go on sale?**  
A: Most launch between late August and early September, with limited editions often selling out by mid-October. Early-bird discounts are common, so signing up for brand newsletters in July is a smart move.

**Q: Are premium-priced calendars worth the investment?**  
A: Absolutely—if you value quality over quantity. Calendars like the artisanal chocolate or beauty options typically retail for $150–$200 AUD when purchased individually, making the $40–$60 price point a genuine steal.

**Q: Can I gift these to friends overseas?**  
A: Yes, though shipping costs and customs duties may apply. Many Australian brands offer international delivery or digital gift options for experiential calendars (like beauty samples or snack boxes), so check the retailer’s policy before purchasing.

**Q: How do I know which calendar suits my household best?**  
A: Match it to your lifestyle. If you’re minimising clutter, lean toward the sustainable or interlocking brick options. For indulgence without guilt, the chocolate or beauty calendars deliver curated experiences. Kids’ calendars thrive on variety and durability.

**Q: Do the contents expire or lose value if opened late?**  
A: Food-based calendars should be consumed within 3–6 months of opening for best quality. Non-perishable items (skincare, toys, bricks) are designed to be used at your own pace, though some beauty products carry a 12-month shelf life once exposed to air.
