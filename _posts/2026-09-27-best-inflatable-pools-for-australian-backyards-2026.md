### FAQ: Your Inflatable Pool Questions Answered

**Q1: How long does a quality inflatable pool actually last in the Australian climate?**
With proper care and UV protection, a high-quality 600D inflatable pool can easily last three to five seasons. The key is storing it in a cool, dry place when not in use and avoiding sharp objects or extreme heat on the material during peak summer months.

**Q2: What's the best way to fill these pools quickly without wasting water?**
An automatic electric pump is a game-changer, filling most 10ft pools in under an hour. To save on water bills, look for council hose water rebates or use a timer on your tap. Filling early in the morning also reduces evaporation loss before the sun gets too high.

**Q3: Can I use saltwater in my inflatable pool?**
No, you should never use saltwater in an inflatable pool as it can degrade the PVC and nylon materials over time, leading to leaks. Stick to fresh water and use a proper chlorination system or shock treatment if you want to keep the water sanitised for extended family use.

**Q4: How much does it cost to fill an inflatable pool?**
While prices vary by region, filling a 10ft pool typically costs between $5 and $10 in water rates depending on your council's tiered pricing. Many families find that the cost is negligible compared to daily swimming lessons or public pool entry fees, making it a highly budget-friendly summer activity.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do modern inflatable pools actually last?**  
With proper care, a quality model will typically last three to five seasons. Reinforced PVC layers and anti-tear stitching in 2026 designs significantly outperform older generations, especially when stored dry between uses.

**Do I need an electric pump, or will a manual one work?**  
Manual pumps are fine for pools under 10ft, but anything larger will save you serious arm fatigue with a battery-powered or dual-chamber air pump. Most top picks now feature standard pool-compatible valves that work across brands.

**Can I leave the water in during winter or between uses?**  
It’s not recommended. Standing water breeds algae and bacteria, and temperature swings can stress seams. Drain completely, scrub lightly if needed, dry thoroughly, and store indoors or under a breathable cover for maximum lifespan.

**Are inflatable pools safe for toddlers and young children?**  
Yes, provided you follow basic water safety protocols: constant supervision, non-slip interior mats, and models with gradual entry slopes. The Bestway 10ft and Lakeside 9ft both feature rounded corners and reinforced floors that make them excellent starter picks for little ones.

**How do I keep the water fresh without heavy chemicals?**  
Skip the chlorine overload. A simple pool skimmer, a floating filter bag, and weekly partial water changes (draining 25% and refilling) will keep things clean. Many families also use gentle ozone purifiers or saltwater conversion kits that are kinder to skin and pool materials.
