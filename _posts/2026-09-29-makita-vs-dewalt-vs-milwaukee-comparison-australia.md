### **FAQ**

**Does the Makita XFD10R come with a battery and charger?**  
No, this model is sold as a "body only" unit. You'll need to pair it with your existing Makita 18V LXT batteries or purchase a starter kit separately. This keeps the upfront cost lower if you're already invested in the ecosystem.

**Will it work with older Makita 18V LXT batteries?**  
Yes, the XFD10R is fully compatible with all Makita 18V LXT lithium-ion batteries, regardless of capacity or generation. I recommend a 4.0Ah or higher pack for optimal runtime on heavier tasks.

**Is this drill powerful enough for professional trades?**  
While it's optimised for serious DIYers and weekend warriors, its brushless motor and durable clutch handle light-to-moderate trade work like cabinet installation, drywall hanging, and furniture assembly. For high-frequency concrete drilling or structural framing, you'll want to step up to Makita's professional-grade LXT range or the XGT platform.

**Where can I purchase it in Australia?**  
You can find the Makita XFD10R at major hardware retailers like Bunnings Warehouse, Mitre 10, and online through Makita’s official Australian store, Amazon AU, and Jaycar. Prices fluctuate, so keep an eye on seasonal sales or trade discount programs.
