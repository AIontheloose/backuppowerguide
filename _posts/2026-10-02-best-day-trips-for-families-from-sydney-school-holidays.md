### FAQ: Your Day Trip Questions Answered

**Q: What's the best season for family day trips from Sydney?**
A: Autumn and Spring are my top picks for comfortable walking weather, but winter offers cozy bush walks and summer is perfect for beaches and waterfalls. Just pack layers and sun protection!

**Q: Do I need to book everything in advance for 2026 holidays?**
A: Yes! Popular spots fill up fast during school breaks. Book parking, ferry tickets, and entry passes early to secure the best rates and avoid disappointment.

**Q: What if it rains? Are there backup plans?**
A: Absolutely. Sydney's got hidden gems for wet days too. Consider the Australian National Maritime Museum in Darling Harbour, indoor play centres in Penrith, or cozy cafes in the Blue Mountains that double as warming stations.

**Q: Which trips are best for toddlers versus teens?**
A: Toddlers and little ones often love Taronga Zoo and the Manly ferry ride. Teens might prefer the Scenic World cable cars in the Blue Mountains or hiking trails in the Royal National Park where they can burn off energy.
