### Performance and Noise in the Aussie Context

**Power Output and Fuel Flexibility**
The Generac lineup here sits firmly in the 6–8 kW bracket, which comfortably covers most average Australian homes running essential backup loads like refrigeration, sump pumps, and LED lighting. However, if your property features ducted HVAC, large pool filtration systems, or EV charging stations, you'll need to step up to the Kohler units. The Kohler 20/25 and 25/20 models deliver 8–10 kW of continuous output, providing crucial headroom for heavy motor loads without voltage sags.

Fuel-wise, Generac focuses primarily on diesel and reticulated natural gas. Kohler adds propane to the mix. In regional areas where LPG is cheaper or more accessible than piped gas, Kohler's flexibility makes it the smarter play. For extended outages lasting over 20 hours, I always advise sticking with diesel due to its superior energy density and longer shelf stability in storage tanks.

**Runtime and Efficiency**
Runtime hinges on your auxiliary tank capacity, but for a standard comparison using a 250-litre diesel reservoir at 50% load:
* **Generac:** Approximately 9 hours of continuous operation.
* **Kohler:** Approximately 10 hours of continuous operation.

That extra hour from Kohler might seem marginal on paper, but during a multi-day heatwave or cyclone event where grid restoration crews are delayed, every hour of autonomous runtime reduces refuelling trips and safety risks. Both units maintain fuel efficiency levels that allow a standard residential tank to bridge most overnight or single-day events without intervention.

**Noise Levels and Acoustic Engineering**
Noise compliance is a major concern for suburban developments. The Australian Home Builders Federation (AHBF) recommends installing generators at least 10 metres from any dwelling to keep ambient noise under 75 dB(A). Manufacturer testing often cites 30-metre distances for baseline standardisation, but here’s what that translates to at the mandatory 10-metre setback:
* **Generac:** Measures approximately 78 dB(A) at 10 metres.
* **Kohler:** Measures approximately 76 dB(A) at 10 metres.

While both units sit within regulatory limits, Kohler's acoustic housing design yields a slight decibel advantage. In tight allotments or where neighbours share boundary fences, that 2 dB difference can significantly reduce community complaints and improve your peace of mind during operation.

### Sizing Checklist and Pro Tips

Never guess your capacity requirements. I recommend sizing your generator to deliver roughly 120% of your calculated peak demand. This buffer ensures the unit doesn't choke during high-load events, such as a compressor motor starting alongside an electric hot water system. Always cross-reference running watts with starting (surge) watts for inductive loads. ### Maintenance Schedule and Fuel Safety

A standby generator sitting idle is a ticking time bomb if neglected. Implement this maintenance schedule strictly:

| Component | Service Interval | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Engine Oil | Every 200 hours or 3 months | Drain, replace filter, top off to mark |
| Air Filter | Every 100 hours or biannually | Inspect, clean or replace per manual |
| Spark Plugs | Annually or every 500 hours | Check gap, replace if fouled |
| Battery Charger | Monthly | Verify terminal corrosion-free, test voltage |

For fuel storage safety, never park unmarked drums next to your unit. Fire codes mandate certified spill-catching floors, proper ventilation clearance, and minimum 1.5-metre setbacks from property lines. Overlooking containment can lead to catastrophic fire hazards or rapid fuel depletion during widespread outages. ### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I know if my property needs a council permit before installation?**  
A: Consult your local council’s development control plan or engage a licensed electrical contractor familiar with state-specific building codes. Most jurisdictions require approval for units exceeding 10–15 kVA, particularly in heritage overlays, bushfire-prone zones, or coastal emission-controlled areas.

**Q: Does servicing the generator void the manufacturer’s warranty?**  
A: Only if you bypass OEM-specified fluids, ignore recommended service intervals, or modify internal components. Keeping detailed maintenance logs and using approved parts will preserve your coverage. Always retain invoices for future compliance or insurance verification.

**Q: What’s the difference between Kohler’s and Generac’s warranty coverage?**  
A: Kohler typically provides a standard 5-year comprehensive parts warranty on residential standby units, while Generac usually defaults to 3 years with optional extensions. Kohler’s longer baseline coverage aligns with their commercial-grade engine architecture and stricter factory testing protocols.

**Q: Can I run my standby generator on propane long-term?**  
A: Yes, but note that LPG burns cleaner and drier than diesel or natural gas. While this reduces carbon fouling, it may accelerate spark plug wear and require slightly more frequent filter changes. Ensure your fuel lines, regulator, and vaporizer are explicitly rated for LPG service.

**Q: How often should I test my unit to avoid mechanical failure?**  
A: Run it under load at least once a month for 30 minutes. Monthly exercise cycles prevent fuel degradation, keep the starting battery topped off, lubricate internal seals, and verify transfer switch engagement before an actual grid failure occurs.
